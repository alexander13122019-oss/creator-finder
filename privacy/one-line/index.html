One Line Privacy Policy

This policy applies specifically to the One Line Android application published by XOTOX STUDIO.

LAST UPDATED · 24 AUGUST 2026PACKAGE · com.xotoxstudio.onelineXOTOX STUDIO

1. Introduction

One Line is an Android application developed and published by XOTOX STUDIO. This policy explains the currently documented handling of information in the app.

2. Information You Provide

The user may enter short personal text entries and related preferences. These entries are provided voluntarily for personal record-keeping.

3. Local Data Storage

Personal entries and app preferences are kept locally on the device according to the currently documented app design. The app's main functionality does not require a XOTOX STUDIO account. No cloud synchronisation of this app content is described in the currently available project information.

4. Advertising

The free version may use Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Where advertising is enabled, Google may process information needed to display ads, measure performance, prevent fraud or abuse, and manage consent or advertising choices according to region and device settings.

AdMob is a third-party service provided by Google. XOTOX STUDIO does not sell the personal app content described in this policy.

5. Purchases

One Line may offer an optional one-time PRO purchase through Google Play. Google Play processes the transaction; XOTOX STUDIO does not directly receive or store complete card or payment details. The app may query Google Play to verify or restore the PRO entitlement.

6. Third-Party Services

Depending on the version and enabled features, One Line may use Google Mobile Ads, Google consent services, and Google Play Billing. These services process information under Google's own terms and policies.

See the Google Privacy Policy and Google's AdMob privacy information.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Local app information remains on the device until the user deletes it through an available Delete or Clear function, clears the application's data in Android settings, or uninstalls the application. Clearing app data or uninstalling may permanently remove local information. Information processed by Google is retained according to Google's policies and the user's applicable settings.

8. Children's Privacy

One Line is not specifically directed to children. XOTOX STUDIO does not knowingly use the app to request personal information from children through an account or XOTOX STUDIO backend. Parents or guardians may contact XOTOX STUDIO with privacy questions.

9. Security

One Line limits remote handling of the personal app content described above by keeping it locally according to the currently documented design. No storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure; users should maintain appropriate device security.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated if One Line's features, SDKs, data handling, or applicable requirements change. The latest revision date appears at the top of this page.

11. Contact

For privacy questions about One Line, contact XOTOX STUDIO at xotoxoficial@gmail.com.

Scope. This policy is for One Line only. Other XOTOX STUDIO products have separate privacy information in the Privacy centre.
