Soundscape support

Help with Soundscape.

For playback, favourites, mixes, advertising, consent, or Pro purchase questions.

Before contacting support

Include the app name “Soundscape”, Android version, device model, and clear steps to reproduce the issue.

Local data

Soundscape has no XOTOX STUDIO account or backend. Favourites, mixes, recent history, preferences, unlocks, and appearance are stored locally.

Purchases

Google Play processes the lifetime soundscape_pro purchase. XOTOX STUDIO does not receive card or bank details.

Contact

Email xotoxoficial@gmail.com with “Soundscape support” in the subject.
